TANKS vs CARS

How to Play

One player vs. CPU. Each round you're randomly assigned the tank or the car.

If you're the Tank

  • ↑ ← ↓ → to drive
  • Space to shoot
  • Goal: shoot down the car before time runs out

If you're the Car

  • ↑ ← ↓ → to drive
  • Goal: survive until the timer hits zero

If you're the Tank

  • Drag the joystick to drive
  • Tap the screen to shoot
  • Goal: shoot down the car before time runs out

If you're the Car

  • Drag the joystick to drive
  • Goal: survive until the timer hits zero

Stay on the ice! Hit the snow and you'll spin out.
